## 📖 What Is Cobblemon?

**Cobblemon** is a Pokémon-style mod for Minecraft Java Edition created by **CobbledStudios**. It's fully open-source and designed to integrate smoothly with vanilla Minecraft and modpacks [citation:3][citation:11].

The mod includes **over 700 Pokémon** from Generations 1–9, competitive battles powered by the **Pokémon Showdown!** engine, and mechanics inspired by **Pokémon Legends: Arceus** — including free movement during battles [citation:3][citation:13].

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **700+ Pokémon** — Generations 1–9 catchable
- ✅ **Pokémon Showdown! engine** — competitive battle mechanics
- ✅ **Free-movement battles** — build, mine, or run during combat
- ✅ **Apricorn farming** — grow and harvest Apricorns for Poké Balls
- ✅ **Fossil revival** — excavate fossils and revive ancient Pokémon
- ✅ **Full multiplayer** — trade and battle with friends
- ✅ **Mod compatibility** — works with Terralith, BYG, and more

> ⚠️ **Cobblemon is not affiliated with Pokémon or Minecraft.** Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang; Pokémon is a trademark of Nintendo, Creatures Inc. [citation:15]

> ⚠️ **Cobblemon is Java Edition only.** It does not work on Bedrock, consoles, or mobile [citation:2][citation:7].

### 2️⃣ Download the `.jar` and dependencies
[![Download from MineMods.top](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOWNLOAD-minemods.top-brightgreen?style=for-the-badge&logo=download&logoColor=white&color=00CED1)](https://minemods.top/)

> ⚠️ **Fabric users:** Also download **Fabric API**.
> ⚠️ **NeoForge users:** Also download **Kotlin for Forge** [citation:3].

### 3️⃣ Drop files into `.minecraft/mods/`

**Windows:**

**macOS:**
```
~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/mods/
```

**Linux:**
```
~/.minecraft/mods/
```

Then launch Minecraft with the **Fabric / NeoForge** profile. Press **M** to open starter selection [citation:12].
